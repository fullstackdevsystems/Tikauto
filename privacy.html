Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikAuto handles information when users connect their TikTok accounts and use the service.

1. Information We May Access

When a user voluntarily connects a TikTok account, TikAuto may access information authorized by the user through TikTok's official APIs.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include:

  • Basic TikTok account information.
  • Authorized public profile information.
  • Information about the user's TikTok posts.
  • Post performance information such as view counts when available.
  • Authorization tokens required to communicate with TikTok APIs.

2. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide TikAuto functionality, including:

  • Connecting an authorized TikTok account.
  • Retrieving authorized post information.
  • Monitoring configured post performance metrics.
  • Managing the user's content workflow.
  • Preparing content for future publication.

3. TikTok Passwords

TikAuto does not request or store the user's TikTok password. Authentication is performed through TikTok's official authorization process.

4. Data Sharing

TikAuto does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform functionality explicitly authorized by the user.

5. Data Storage

TikAuto may store configuration information, content queue information, publication history and authorization credentials necessary to operate the service.

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect stored information.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide TikAuto functionality or comply with applicable requirements.

7. Revoking Access

Users may revoke TikAuto's access to their TikTok account through TikTok authorization settings where supported.

8. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their TikAuto account or connection. TikAuto will process valid requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

TikAuto integrates with TikTok services. TikTok's own privacy practices are governed by TikTok's policies and are separate from this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as TikAuto evolves. The latest version will always be published on this page.

11. Contact

Privacy-related questions or data requests may be directed to the TikAuto application administrator.
