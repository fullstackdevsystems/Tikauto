Content workflow for TikTok creators

Manage your TikTok content workflow

TikAuto helps creators organize their content queue, monitor the performance of their latest TikTok posts and prepare future publications through a simple and controlled workflow.

What TikAuto Does

Post Monitoring

Monitor performance information for authorized TikTok posts, including view activity when available through the TikTok API.

Content Queue

Organize images and content that creators plan to publish and prevent previously processed content from being unintentionally reused.

Publication Preparation

Prepare captions, hashtags and publication information before content is submitted to TikTok.

Secure Connection

TikAuto connects to TikTok using official authorization methods. Users do not provide their TikTok password directly to TikAuto.

How It Works

1. Connect your TikTok account

The creator authorizes TikAuto through TikTok's official authentication process.

2. Add content

Creators organize content that they want to prepare for future TikTok publications.

3. Monitor performance

TikAuto can retrieve authorized post information through TikTok's official APIs.

4. Prepare the next publication

When configured conditions are met, TikAuto can prepare the next item in the creator's content queue for publication.
